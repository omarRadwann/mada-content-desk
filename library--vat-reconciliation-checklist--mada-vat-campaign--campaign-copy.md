## English LinkedIn adaptation

“The VAT figures do not match” is an observation, not an explanation. Start with the comparison scope, the source behind each figure and the evidence behind each difference. Our new guide explains five practical steps and a worked example, including a hypothetical example, to make the review conversation more useful. It does not determine the tax treatment of an individual case.

Read: https://mada-adv.com/insights/vat-reconciliation-checklist/

## Short video script — 30 seconds, vertical

0–5 seconds: Show two abstract report cards. Spoken: «أرقام ضريبة القيمة المضافة لا تتطابق؟ وجود فرق وحده لا يشرح السبب».
5–12 seconds: Reveal three labelled cards: النطاق، المصدر، الفرق. Spoken: «تأكد أولًا من نطاق المقارنة، ثم اربط كل رقم بمصدره».
12–21 seconds: Highlight a simple difference register. Spoken: «سجّل الفرق، وما تحققتم منه، والسؤال الذي يحتاج إلى توضيح. لا تغيّر رقمًا فقط لتتساوى الإجماليات».
21–30 seconds: Show the article title and website. Spoken: «في دليل مدى، خمس خطوات ومثال عملي لتنظيم المراجعة وتجهيز نقاش أوضح. اقرأ الدليل في الموقع».

Use abstract report graphics without client data. Keep captions as editable text and review Arabic timing. This is a production brief, not a rendered video. No spokesperson or actual client is depicted.

## Claim review

Official supporting source: ZATCA VAT Journey, return-filing requirements, https://www.zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/CustomerJourney/Pages/tax-journey.aspx . Supports only the reference to sales and purchase data in filing requirements. The five-step workflow and example is Mada's own organisational suggestion. The SAR 12,000 / 11,500 example is explicitly hypothetical and has no tax-rate, liability or filing conclusion.

No claims about filing deadlines, correction thresholds, recoverability, savings or guaranteed compliance are made. Technical case-specific follow-up requires professional review. Topic demand is a hypothesis, not a search-volume finding.
