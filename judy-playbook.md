# Judy publishing playbook

1. Select the current campaign and complete numbered image set.
2. Read the channel-specific caption and article together.
3. Confirm the technical source scope and CEO approval.
4. Test the live article destination and language. A prepared URL is not publication evidence.
5. Check Arabic, image crops, sequence and alternative text.
6. For Instagram, verify the profile or Story link route. For LinkedIn, use the article link directly when appropriate.
7. Keep paid ads on hold. Do not treat a calendar entry as permission to spend.
8. Record the actual published post URL and assign comment follow-up. Never request tax files in public comments.
9. Compare article visits, useful engagement and qualified enquiries separately. Posting time and cadence are hypotheses to test.
