# Zakat review file — refreshed campaign

Status: redesigned creative prepared for Omar/CEO review; no social publishing or paid delivery performed.

## Purpose
Help finance teams stop circulating ambiguous “final” versions. The viewer learns how to check scope, identify a version and assign an open question. These are original organisational suggestions, not a list of statutory attachments or a zakat calculation.

## Carousel order
1. Three files labelled final: which do we share?
2. A hypothetical comparison: all branches/full period versus one branch/part of the period.
3. A hypothetical internal file name with version, status and coordinator.
4. Turn “the figures differ” into a specific question with an owner and follow-up status.
5. Check scope, version and open questions before sending.
6. Save the checklist; read the full Mada guide.

«نهائي»، «نهائي جديد»، «آخر نسخة»… المشكلة ليست في عدد الملفات، بل في وضوح النسخة التي سيعتمد عليها الفريق.

قبل مشاركة ملف منشأتك للمراجعة الزكوية:
١. تحقّق من النطاق: هل يغطي الملف المنشأة والفروع والفترة المقصودة؟
٢. حدّد الإصدار وحالته: تحت المراجعة أم معتمد داخليًا؟ واذكر مسؤول التنسيق.

هذه إرشادات لتنظيم العمل، ولا تحدّد متطلبات منشأتك أو المعالجة الزكوية المناسبة لها.

## English adaptation
Three files labelled “final” are not a handover process. Before sharing a business file for zakat review, define its scope, identify its version and internal status, and give each open question an owner.

For example, an all-branch report covering a full period may differ from a single-branch report covering part of that period. Establish the scope before comparing the figures. This is a hypothetical organisational example, not a tax calculation.

Save the checklist for your next file-preparation session. These suggestions do not establish your business’s filing requirements or zakat treatment.

Guide: https://mada-adv.com/insights/zakat-file-preparation/
